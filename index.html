Tomecraft

Prototype: combat (M1) and collecting familiars (M2). Equip any three familiars you have bound. Each one gives you one ability. Every attack goes exactly where you aim: there is no aim assist.

Equip three familiars

Controls

PhoneLeft thumb: move. Right thumb: aim and fire. Ability buttons: tap to cast where you're aiming, or drag from the button to aim that ability. Placed abilities land where you drag to.
KeyboardWASD move · mouse aim · hold left click to fire · Q / E / R abilities (hold for Charged Blast) · Space dodge

Before you explore

Tomecraft uses your phone's location to place familiars around you. Your location stays on this device and is never sent anywhere.

Stay safe: watch where you're walking, keep to public places and paths, don't trespass, and never play while driving or cycling. Familiar interactions pause when you're moving fast.
